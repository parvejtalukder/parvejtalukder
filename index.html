About Me

Hello! I am Parvej Husen Talukder, a Bangladeshi poet, children’s writer, and freelance journalist. I am the founder of Kavya Kishor and known as the "King Rhymester of the Haor Region." My works focus on the beauty of nature, the emotions of life, and the intricacies of the human spirit.
